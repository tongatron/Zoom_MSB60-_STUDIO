    Privacy

    Without an account

    You can use the whole site without signing in. Then everything you make stays in this browser's storage (localStorage) and never reaches the server: your patches (My patches), the patch you are editing, advice settings, rule packs, the view you chose (list or grid), the backups made by the Pedal manager, the optional local links between pedal memories and My patches, the preference for being asked about pedal changes and the list of pedal memories as it last read them (shown greyed while the pedal is not connected), and your choice on the sign-up reminder: Ignore this message is remembered in the same way, while Maybe later is remembered only until you close the tab. It is kept until you clear the site data of this browser; a private window, another browser or another device won't have it.

    A link that opens a patch in the builder (from Tone ideas or the Pedal manager, or a share link made earlier) carries the patch inside the link itself: the server does not store it.

    With an account

    An account keeps the same data on the server, so it is safe if the browser is cleared and it follows you on every device where you sign in. The server stores:

    • your email address and, if you give one, a name;
    • your password, only as a salted PBKDF2 hash (nobody can read it back);
    • when you signed up, when you last signed in and whether you confirmed your email;
    • for each pedal: your patches, deleted-patch and deleted-backup markers (only an id and a date, kept 90 days so other devices delete them too), advice settings, view, the patch you are editing, rule packs and Pedal manager backups.

    Signing in sets one cookie, zms_session: it only keeps you signed in (30 days), it is not used for tracking. One-time links (password reset, email confirmation) are stored only as a hash and expire after one hour or 24 hours.

    Emails and notices

    The site sends you an email when you create an account (with an optional link to confirm your address) and when you ask for a new password. Emails are sent through Google's Gmail. When someone creates an account, the site administrator receives a Telegram message with the new email address. Report a problem (and Contact us on the landing page and on the MS-50G+ help page) also reach the administrator as a Telegram message: it holds what you write, your name if you give one and your email (optional for a problem report, needed for Contact us, and used only to reply), the address of the page, the pedal, the date of the site's last update, the size of your window and the name of your browser (these technical details are sent only while the box "Send technical details" is ticked), and the email of your account if you are signed in. The site does not keep these messages; they stay in the administrator's Telegram chat. There is no newsletter and your address is never shared with anyone else.

    Where the data is

    On a server run by tongatron.org, reached through Cloudflare (which carries the connection but does not store your data). The database is copied every day to a backup on the same server; backups are kept 30 days, then deleted.

    Page views are counted with privacy-friendly analytics (Umami, hosted by tongatron.org), without cookies and without building a profile of you.

    Your choices

    • Download your data: on the account page, as a JSON file.
    • Delete your account: on the account page. The account and everything in it are deleted at once; copies in the daily backups disappear within 30 days.
    • Sign out: by default it also removes your data from that browser (it stays in your account).
    • Data in this browser only: clear the site data in your browser settings.

    Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub or write to the site administrator through tongatron.org.
